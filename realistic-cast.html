What "realistic" means here: this is the realistic ceiling for runtime vector art — the medium Rive renders inside Flutter. Naturalistic proportions, layered gradient shading, fur and feather edges, true animal eyes. Going further (photoreal fur, real lighting) is not a style choice, it's a different pipeline — pre-rendered images or the 3D route we already priced. Worth knowing before choosing: every category leader tested with 3–6 year-olds ships the cartoon style, and the research says warmth and reactions carry attachment, not fidelity.
