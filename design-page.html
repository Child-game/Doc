Critter Camp.

A learning app for 3–6 year-olds. One friendly fox, a winding road of little wins, and a voice that talks to kids who can't read yet. This page shows how it looks, moves, and sounds.

The win

~1.5s of joy: jump, one sticker earned, back to the road. Short on purpose — celebration never outshines learning.

How Pip stays alive

The mascot above is running the real spec, in this page. Three layers, authored once in Rive, running under every state.

3.6s Breathing

Chest scales up 1–2%, shoulders and head follow a beat later — the offset is what reads as alive. First and last frame identical, so the loop never pops.

runs under every other state

3–5s Blink

A quick eyelid close on a randomised timer — never synced to the breath, or the loop becomes visible. Built as a simple shape swap, not an eye rig.

random interval, never on the loop

30–60s Fidgets

Ear flick, head tilt, tail swish, a look off-screen — 3–5 of them, picked at random, blended in over 0.2s. A lone idle loop is noticed in ~90 seconds; fidgets hide it.

kids replay far more than adults

Duolingo-style: the app fires named triggers, Rive plays the feeling. Every state blends back to idle.

There is no fail pose anywhere in the rig. A wrong tap gets encouragement and an easier retry — by design, not by accident.

How the voice works

Our users can't read, so audio is the interface. One warm human voice — recorded slow, warm, and a little over-the-top.

Three channels, one rule

VOICE
SFX
MUSIC
  • Voice always wins — music ducks under it, sound effects never cut it off.
  • Every line for the current chapter is preloaded. A late voice line feels broken to a four-year-old.
  • While a line plays, isSpeaking is on and Pip's jaw follows the loudness — no lip-sync tech needed at this size.
  • Plain .m4a files named vo_<level>_<line>.m4a, mapped from the level JSON.

Rules we never break

Candy Crush gave us the road. We deliberately left its economy behind — those mechanics work by exploiting things a four-year-old can't defend against.

✕ Lives, timers, streaks, scores, stars
✓ Retries are free, forever, for everything
✕ Levels that get harder after failure
✓Levels that get easier — fewer choices, stronger hint
✕ Anything that takes progress away
✓ Stickers only ever add up
✕ Ads, IAP prompts, links a child can reach
✓ Grown-up things behind a worded-maths gate
✕ Drag, pinch, long-press, swipe
✓ Tap only · ≥120pt targets · taps debounced
